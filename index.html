About Me

I build spoken dialogue models that listen while speakinglisten while speaking, think while talking, keep working while chatting, and know when to speak.

I am a second-year M.S. student in Electronic Information (Artificial Intelligence) at Zhejiang University, and currently a research intern at Tencent Hunyuan, working on post-training of speech models. Previously, I interned at Alibaba Tongyi Lab, and received my B.Eng. from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT).

My research focuses on spoken dialogue models, especially their post-training, full-duplex interaction, and multimodal agents. So far I have co-authored 18 papers, including 13 at top conferences such as ACL, EMNLP, ICLR, ECCV and AAAI — 6 of them as first or co-first author, with 2 ACL main conference orals.

Gander: Multimodal Duplex Interaction Agent

Orantqing, Shengpeng Ji, Junlong Tong, Jialong Zuo, Dongjie Fu, Di Cao, Yangzhuo Li, Shangda Wu, et al.

Technical Report, arXiv 2026

An open-source native duplex interaction model with an asynchronous agent loop. A cerebellum–brain architecture pairs a streaming Thinker-Talker with an asynchronous reasoning agent, unifying continuous multimodal perception, full-duplex conversation and long-horizon task execution — users can interrupt, redirect or share their screen while work continues.
